# SEC RAG Benchmark & Modeling Questions (55 Questions)
# These questions are designed to test Single-Hop, Multi-Hop, and Contradiction Retrieval in SEC Filings.

## Part 1: Single-Hop Factual Queries (Direct Retrieval)
1. What was Apple's total net sales for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2023?
2. How much did Microsoft report in Cloud Revenue for Q3 2023?
3. What is the stated par value of Tesla's common stock as of their latest 10-K?
4. Who is listed as the independent registered public accounting firm for Alphabet Inc. in 2023?
5. What was Amazon's total operating income for the North America segment in Q4 2023?
6. How many full-time employees did Meta Platforms report having at the end of fiscal year 2023?
7. What was the total amount of cash and cash equivalents held by Nvidia as of January 28, 2024?
8. Did Netflix report any material changes to their risk factors in their Q2 2023 10-Q?
9. What was the total research and development (R&D) expense for Pfizer in 2023?
10. How much debt did Chevron issue during the fiscal year 2023?
11. What is the effective tax rate reported by Pfizer for the first quarter of 2024?
12. What was the total revenue for Google's Google Cloud segment in 2023?
13. How much did Johnson & Johnson spend on sales, marketing, and administrative expenses in Q1 2024?
14. What are the primary products listed in Nvidia's Data Center segment?
15. What was the basic earnings per share (EPS) for UnitedHealth in fiscal year 2024?

## Part 2: Multi-Hop & Comparative Queries (Cross-Quarter/Cross-Entity)
16. How did Apple's iPhone revenue in Q4 2024 compare to iPhone revenue in Q4 2023?
17. What was the year-over-year percentage growth in Microsoft's Azure revenue from Q1 2023 to Q1 2024?
18. Compare the total R&D spending of Alphabet and Meta Platforms for the fiscal year 2023.
19. Did Tesla's automotive gross margin increase or decrease between Q2 2023 and Q3 2023, and by how much?
20. What was the sequential change in Amazon's AWS operating income from Q2 2023 to Q3 2023?
21. Contrast the primary risk factors related to artificial intelligence mentioned in Microsoft's 10-K versus Google's (Alphabet) 10-K.
22. How much did Nvidia's Data Center revenue grow from fiscal year 2023 to fiscal year 2024?
23. What were the total dividends paid by JPMorgan Chase in 2023 compared to 2024?
24. How did the operating margin of Amazon's North America segment compare to its International segment in Q4 2023?
25. Did Netflix's total global streaming paid memberships grow more in Q1 2024 or Q4 2023?
26. Compare the total capital expenditures of ExxonMobil and Chevron for the fiscal year 2023.
27. How much did total operating expenses change for Netflix from Q3 2023 to Q3 2024?
28. What was the difference in total liabilities reported by JPMorgan Chase at the end of 2023 versus the end of 2024?
29. Did Tesla's vehicle deliveries increase or decrease YoY in Q2 2023, and what was the impact on automotive revenues?
30. How did the revenue composition (Products vs Services) change for Apple between 2023 and 2024?

## Part 4: Guidance, Forward-Looking & Contradiction Queries
46. Did management provide specific revenue guidance for Q2 2024 in the Q1 2024 10-Q, and does it align with previous statements?
47. Did the CEO's statement about supply chain normalization in Q1 contradict the risk factors listed in the Q2 10-Q regarding component shortages?
48. In 2023, Apple stated they would halt stock repurchases, but did they report repurchasing stock in the 2024 10-K?
49. Compare the projected capital expenditure for 2024 mentioned in the Q3 2023 filing versus the actual capital expenditure guidance in the 2023 10-K.
50. Did Apple's stated goal of achieving carbon neutrality by 2030 change between their 2023 and 2024 filings?
51. Is there a discrepancy between the reported restructuring costs in the Q2 10-Q and the cumulative restructuring costs reported in the year-end 10-K?
52. Did the management's discussion on international expansion plans in Q1 match the actions reported in the Q3 filing?
53. In the 2023 10-K, Apple cited a specific competitor as their main threat. Was this changed or updated in the 2024 10-K?
54. Did the projected timeline for the new manufacturing facility completion change between the Q1 2023 and Q4 2023 filings?
55. How did the company's outlook on consumer spending change from the first half of 2023 to the second half?
